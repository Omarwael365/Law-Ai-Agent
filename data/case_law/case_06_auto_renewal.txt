CASE NO. 2023-CV-05512
NIMBLEOPS INC. v. ENTERPRISECLOUD SOLUTIONS LLC
United States District Court, District of Delaware
Filed: April 18, 2023

OPINION AND ORDER

BACKGROUND

NimbleOps Inc. ("NimbleOps"), a startup with 45 employees, subscribed to EnterpriseCloud Solutions LLC's ("EnterpriseCloud") SaaS platform for project management in January 2021. The subscription was for a two-year term at $60,000 per year.

NimbleOps's CEO electronically signed a 40-page Master Subscription Agreement ("MSA") via DocuSign on January 5, 2021. Section 12.4 of the MSA, located on page 34, stated:

"Automatic Renewal. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods at the then-current list price unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term."

In January 2023, NimbleOps was charged $120,000 for a third year (the price had doubled). NimbleOps claims it was unaware of the auto-renewal clause.

ANALYSIS

I. Enforceability of the Auto-Renewal Clause

Under general contract law, an auto-renewal clause is enforceable if it was part of a validly formed contract and the subscribing party had reasonable notice. NimbleOps is a business entity, and its CEO signed the agreement. Courts hold that a party who signs a contract is bound by its terms even if unread.

II. Electronic Signature Validity

Under the E-SIGN Act (15 U.S.C. Section 7001) and UETA, electronic signatures are legally equivalent to handwritten signatures. The DocuSign audit trail confirmed the CEO accessed every page.

III. Price Increase Upon Renewal

The clause permits renewal at "then-current list price." However, a 100% price increase without advance notice raises procedural unconscionability concerns. The court finds the renewal enforceable at the original $60,000 rate.

ORDER

1. The auto-renewal clause is ENFORCEABLE as to the renewal mechanism.
2. The electronic signature is VALID.
3. Renewal price is reduced to $60,000. EnterpriseCloud shall refund $60,000.

SO ORDERED.
