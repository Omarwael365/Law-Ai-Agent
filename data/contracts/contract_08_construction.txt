AIA DOCUMENT A101 — STANDARD FORM OF AGREEMENT
BETWEEN OWNER AND CONTRACTOR

This Agreement is entered into as of February 1, 2021, by and between:

Greenfield Properties LLC ("Owner")
and
Summit Builders Inc. ("Contractor")

ARTICLE 1 — THE WORK
1.1 The Contractor shall perform all Work required by the Contract Documents for the construction of a 45,000 square-foot commercial office building located at 1200 Market Street, Denver, Colorado (the "Project").

ARTICLE 2 — CONTRACT SUM
2.1 The Contract Sum is Five Million Dollars ($5,000,000.00), subject to adjustments as provided in the Contract Documents.
2.2 The Contract Sum is a Stipulated Sum (fixed price).

ARTICLE 3 — CONTRACT TIME
3.1 Work shall commence within 10 days of Notice to Proceed.
3.2 Substantial Completion: December 31, 2021.
3.3 Final Completion: January 31, 2022.

ARTICLE 4 — PAYMENT
4.1 Monthly progress payments based on percentage of Work completed.
4.2 Retainage: 10% held until Substantial Completion.
4.3 Final payment within 30 days of Final Completion.

ARTICLE 5 — SCOPE OF WORK
5.1 General construction per architectural drawings A-100 through A-450.
5.2 Standard HVAC installation per mechanical specifications M-100.
5.3 Standard electrical per electrical specifications E-100.
5.4 Standard facade: aluminum curtain wall system per A-300.

ARTICLE 7 — CHANGES IN THE WORK
7.1 The Owner may order changes in the Work within the general scope of the Contract.
7.2 A Change Order is a written instrument prepared by the Architect and signed by the Owner, Contractor, and Architect stating their agreement upon: (a) a change in the Work; (b) the amount of adjustment in the Contract Sum; and (c) the extent of adjustment in the Contract Time.
7.3 Construction Change Directives may be issued by the Owner without Contractor agreement on price, subject to later resolution.
7.4 Minor changes in the Work not involving adjustment to Contract Sum or Time may be issued by the Architect.
7.5 All change orders must be documented in writing and approved before work commences.

ARTICLE 8 — CLAIMS AND DISPUTES
8.1 Claims shall be initiated within 21 days of the event giving rise to the claim.
8.2 Disputes shall first be submitted to the Architect for initial decision.
8.3 Unresolved disputes shall proceed to mediation, then binding arbitration.

ARTICLE 9 — INSURANCE AND BONDS
9.1 Contractor shall maintain general liability insurance of $2,000,000 per occurrence.
9.2 Performance and payment bonds required.

ARTICLE 10 — GOVERNING LAW
10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.
