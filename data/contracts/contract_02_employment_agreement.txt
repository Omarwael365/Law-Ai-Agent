EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2019, by and between:

TechStart Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Company")
and
Sarah Chen ("Employee")

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
1.1 Employee is hired as Senior Software Engineer.
1.2 Employee shall report to the VP of Engineering.
1.3 Employee shall perform duties as reasonably assigned.

2. COMPENSATION
2.1 Base Salary: $185,000 per year, paid bi-weekly.
2.2 Equity: 10,000 stock options vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff.
2.3 Benefits: Standard company benefits package including health, dental, and 401(k).

3. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT
3.1 Employment is at-will. Either party may terminate at any time with or without cause.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
4.1 Employee shall not disclose Confidential Information during or after employment.
4.2 Confidential Information includes source code, algorithms, customer data, business strategies, and any proprietary information.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT
5.1 Employee assigns to Company all Inventions conceived during employment.
5.2 "Inventions" means any ideas, inventions, algorithms, software, designs, and intellectual property.

6. RETURN OF MATERIALS
6.1 Upon termination, Employee shall return all Company property and Confidential Information.

7. NON-SOLICITATION
7.1 For 12 months after termination, Employee shall not solicit Company employees or customers.

8. NON-COMPETE
8.1 Scope: For a period of two (2) years following termination of employment, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in, be employed by, consult for, or have any interest in any business that competes with the Company's SaaS products anywhere in the United States.
8.2 Acknowledgment: Employee acknowledges that this restriction is reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests.

9. REMEDIES
9.1 Employee acknowledges that breach of Sections 4, 5, 7, or 8 would cause irreparable harm and that the Company shall be entitled to injunctive relief.

10. SEVERABILITY
10.1 If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
15.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
15.2 Any disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Travis County, Texas.
