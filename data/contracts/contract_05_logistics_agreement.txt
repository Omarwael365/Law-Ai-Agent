LOGISTICS SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Logistics Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of June 1, 2021, by and between:

PeakCommerce PLC, a company incorporated in England and Wales ("Customer")
and
RapidShip Logistics Ltd., a company incorporated in England and Wales ("Service Provider")

1. SERVICES
1.1 Service Provider shall provide package delivery services during the peak holiday season (October 1 through December 31, 2021).
1.2 Total package volume: 10,000 packages.
1.3 Delivery timeframes are specified in Schedule 1.

2. SERVICE LEVELS
2.1 On-time delivery rate: minimum 95%.
2.2 Damage rate: maximum 0.5%.
2.3 Customer satisfaction score: minimum 4.2/5.0.

3. PRICING
3.1 Per-package delivery fee: GBP 12.50 for standard delivery, GBP 25.00 for express.
3.2 Volume discounts apply above 5,000 packages as per Schedule 2.

4. INSURANCE
4.1 Service Provider shall maintain cargo insurance covering loss or damage up to GBP 500 per package.

5. REPORTING
5.1 Service Provider shall provide daily delivery status reports.
5.2 Weekly performance reviews shall be conducted during the contract period.

9. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
9.1 The parties acknowledge that late delivery during the peak holiday season will cause Customer significant harm that is difficult to quantify precisely.
9.2 In the event of delay in delivery, the Service Provider shall pay to the Customer liquidated damages in the amount of GBP 50 per package per day of delay, as a genuine pre-estimate of the loss that would be suffered by the Customer.
9.3 Liquidated damages shall be the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for late delivery.
9.4 The maximum aggregate liquidated damages shall not exceed 40% of the total contract value.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
10.1 Subject to Section 9, neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed the total contract value.
10.2 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages except in cases of wilful misconduct.

11. TERMINATION
11.1 Either party may terminate for material breach with 14 days written notice.
11.2 Customer may terminate for convenience with 30 days written notice.

12. GOVERNING LAW
12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.
12.2 Disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
