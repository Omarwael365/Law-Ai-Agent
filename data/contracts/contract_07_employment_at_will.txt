EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 15, 2018, by and between:

SafetyGuard Manufacturing Inc., a Pennsylvania corporation ("Company")
and
Marcus Thompson ("Employee")

1. POSITION
1.1 Employee is hired as Quality Assurance Manager.
1.2 Employee reports to the VP of Operations.

2. COMPENSATION
2.1 Base Salary: $95,000 per year.
2.2 Annual bonus: up to 15% of base salary based on performance.

3. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT
3.1 Employment is at-will and may be terminated by either party at any time, with or without cause or notice.
3.2 Nothing in this Agreement creates a guarantee of continued employment.

4. DUTIES
4.1 Employee shall oversee quality assurance testing of all manufactured products.
4.2 Employee shall ensure compliance with industry safety standards and regulations.
4.3 Employee shall report quality or safety concerns to management.

5. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
5.1 Annual performance reviews shall be conducted each December.
5.2 Performance improvement plans may be issued at the Company's discretion.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
6.1 Employee shall maintain confidentiality of proprietary manufacturing processes, product designs, and business information.

7. WHISTLEBLOWER ACKNOWLEDGMENT
7.1 The Company acknowledges that Employee has the right to report safety concerns, legal violations, or unethical conduct to appropriate authorities without fear of retaliation, as provided by applicable law.

8. TERMINATION
8.1 Company may terminate employment at any time pursuant to at-will doctrine.
8.2 Upon termination, Employee shall receive accrued salary and unused PTO.

9. GOVERNING LAW
9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
