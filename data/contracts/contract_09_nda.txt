NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 10, 2017, by and between:

VelocitySales Inc., an Illinois corporation ("Disclosing Party" or "Company")
and
James Harrington ("Receiving Party" or "Employee")

1. PURPOSE
This Agreement is entered into in connection with Employee's employment as Vice President of Sales.

2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
2.1 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by the Company to Employee, including but not limited to:
(a) Customer lists and customer contact information
(b) Pricing information, pricing strategies, and discount structures
(c) Business strategies, marketing plans, and sales methodologies
(d) Financial data, revenue figures, and projections
(e) Product roadmaps and development plans
(f) Deal pipeline information and sales forecasts
(g) Vendor and supplier information
(h) Any information marked as "Confidential" or "Proprietary"
(i) All proprietary information disclosed during employment

3. OBLIGATIONS
3.1 Employee shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence.
3.2 Employee shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent.
3.3 Employee shall use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing employment duties.
3.4 Employee shall not copy, reproduce, or remove Confidential Information from Company premises except as necessary for employment duties.

4. EXCLUSIONS
4.1 Confidential Information does not include information that:
(a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of Employee
(b) Was known to Employee prior to disclosure by Company
(c) Is independently developed by Employee without use of Confidential Information
(d) Is disclosed pursuant to a court order or legal requirement

5. TERM
5.1 Confidentiality obligations shall survive for two (2) years following termination of employment.
5.2 Trade secret protections shall survive indefinitely as provided by applicable law.

6. RETURN OF MATERIALS
6.1 Upon termination of employment, Employee shall immediately return all documents, files, and materials containing Confidential Information.
6.2 Employee shall permanently delete all electronic copies of Confidential Information from personal devices.

7. REMEDIES
7.1 Employee acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure would cause irreparable harm.
7.2 Company shall be entitled to injunctive relief, actual damages, and attorneys' fees.
7.3 In cases of willful and malicious misappropriation, exemplary damages up to two times actual damages may be awarded.

8. GOVERNING LAW
8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.
