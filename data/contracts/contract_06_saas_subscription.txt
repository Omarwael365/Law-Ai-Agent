MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 5, 2021, by and between:

EnterpriseCloud Solutions LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Provider")
and
NimbleOps Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Customer")

1. SUBSCRIPTION SERVICES
1.1 Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive right to access the EnterpriseCloud Platform.
1.2 Services include project management, team collaboration, and analytics modules.
1.3 Service tier: Enterprise (up to 100 user seats).

2. TERM
2.1 Initial Term: Two (2) years commencing January 5, 2021.
2.2 The Initial Term begins on the Effective Date and ends on January 4, 2023.

3. FEES
3.1 Annual subscription fee: $60,000 per year.
3.2 Fees are due within 30 days of invoice.
3.3 Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
4.1 Provider guarantees 99.9% uptime availability.
4.2 Scheduled maintenance windows: Sundays 2:00-6:00 AM ET.
4.3 Service credits for downtime exceeding SLA thresholds per Schedule B.

5. DATA OWNERSHIP
5.1 Customer retains all rights to Customer Data.
5.2 Provider may use anonymized, aggregated data for product improvement.

6. SECURITY
6.1 Provider implements SOC 2 Type II certified security controls.
6.2 Data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).

7. SUPPORT
7.1 Enterprise support: 24/7 phone and email.
7.2 Response times: Critical (1 hour), High (4 hours), Medium (8 hours), Low (24 hours).

12. TERM AND RENEWAL
12.1 This Agreement is effective for the Initial Term specified in Section 2.
12.2 Early termination by Customer requires payment of remaining fees in the Initial Term.
12.3 Provider may terminate for material breach with 30 days written notice.
12.4 Automatic Renewal. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods at the then-current list price unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. Written notice must be sent via certified mail or email to contracts@enterprisecloud.com.

13. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
13.1 Provider's aggregate liability shall not exceed fees paid in the 12 months preceding the claim.
13.2 Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.

14. GOVERNING LAW
14.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
