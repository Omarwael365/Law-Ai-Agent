DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into as of January 15, 2021, by and between:

FinovaTech GmbH, a company incorporated in Germany ("Controller" or "Customer")
and
CloudSecure Ltd., a company incorporated in Ireland ("Processor" or "Service Provider")

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person as defined in Article 4(1) GDPR.
1.2 "Processing" has the meaning given in Article 4(2) GDPR.
1.3 "Data Subjects" means the individuals whose Personal Data is processed.

2. SCOPE AND PURPOSE
2.1 The Processor shall process Personal Data solely for the purpose of providing cloud hosting and infrastructure services to the Controller.
2.2 Categories of Data Subjects: Customer's end users and customers.
2.3 Types of Personal Data: names, email addresses, account balances, transaction histories, identification documents.

3. PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS
3.1 The Processor shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller.
3.2 The Processor shall ensure persons authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations.
3.3 The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to data subject access requests.

4. SUB-PROCESSORS
4.1 The Processor shall not engage sub-processors without prior written consent of the Controller.
4.2 The Processor shall impose equivalent data protection obligations on any approved sub-processor.

5. DATA TRANSFERS
5.1 Personal Data shall be processed within the European Economic Area unless otherwise agreed.
5.2 Any transfer to a third country requires appropriate safeguards under Chapter V GDPR.

6. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION
6.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay (and no later than 48 hours) after becoming aware of a Personal Data breach.
6.2 Notification shall include the nature of the breach, categories of data affected, estimated number of data subjects, and remedial measures taken.

7. SECURITY MEASURES
7.1 The Processor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including industry-standard security measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorized or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction, or damage.
7.2 Security measures shall include but not be limited to: access controls, network security monitoring, regular vulnerability assessments, and incident response procedures.

8. AUDIT RIGHTS
8.1 The Controller shall have the right to audit the Processor's compliance with this DPA upon 30 days written notice.

9. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
9.1 Upon termination, the Processor shall delete or return all Personal Data within 90 days.

10. TERM AND TERMINATION
10.1 This DPA shall remain in effect for the duration of the underlying services agreement.
10.2 Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days notice.

11. GOVERNING LAW
11.1 This DPA shall be governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany.

12. LIABILITY
12.1 The Processor's aggregate liability under this DPA shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
12.2 Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this DPA.
